## Acceptance decision

The first Quanthus stock-stage research phase is formally converged and closed. The closeout does not
mean every historical stage code is qualified and does not convert any development evidence into
production authority. It means the main architecture question has been answered strongly enough that
continuing taxonomy-first exploration is no longer justified as a prerequisite for Quanthus.

The accepted architecture boundary is:

```text
Authoritative Continuous Facts
          ↓
Governed deterministic propositions
          ↓
Layered Stage semantic / communication surface
          ↓
Expert and multi-expert consumers
```

The superseded architecture is:

```text
Facts → one mutually exclusive Stage code → decision
```

`primary_stage` / top-1 compression is not required by the accepted implementation architecture.
Historical codes remain useful for provenance, research vocabulary and traceability.

## Evidence carried forward

The convergence preserves, without promotion, the following evidence:

1. `P3-STRUCT-002` selected `prefer_continuous_structure_facts` under its frozen development economic
   comparison. It did not prove forward/live economic superiority.
2. `P3-STAGE-027` replaced the flat state-machine implementation assumption with a layered proposition
   portfolio over continuous Facts.
3. `P3-STAGE-028` implemented the Stage Proposition Bundle contract and non-production
   development-shadow surface.
4. `P3-STAGE-029` showed on all ten accepted retrospective Stage10-active observations that layered
   output preserves explicit parent+nested semantics that comparison-only single-code compression can
   elide.
5. `P3-STAGE-030..032` correctly failed closed on strict historical PIT/public-availability evidence
   and stopped the historical source-evidence hunt rather than weakening the PIT definition.
6. `P3-STAGE-033` mechanically passed its consumer packet gate, but the four-role result audit found
   information equivalence unproven. The accepted bounded claim is therefore communication value for
   already-derived Stage10/11 state, not pure layered-presentation causality.

## Final product roles

### Continuous Facts

Continuous Support Evolution, Trend Evolution, Structure Order and related governed Facts remain the
computational and audit substrate. Downstream experts may consume them directly.

### Layered Stage propositions

The Layered Stage Proposition Bundle remains a governed semantic interface for communicating derived
context, condition, modifier, epistemic and control assertions. Stage10 structural-failure context
plus Stage11 nested rebound is the mature development demonstration. The surface remains
non-production unless a later downstream consumer phase separately grants authority.

### Historical vocabulary

Historical codes 0..12 remain traceable research vocabulary. They are not a checklist that must be
completed. Parked, exploratory, modifier, context and nested-condition dispositions are legitimate
final outcomes for this research phase.

## Claims that remain closed

This convergence does not prove or authorize:

- strict historical PIT/as-known validity for the retrospective 2020 evidence;
- information-equivalent Layered-vs-Facts causal superiority;
- predictive utility or economic superiority;
- out-of-sample Stage qualification;
- production expert consumption;
- strategy or trading authority;
- reopening the consumed 2022-2024 arbitration partition;
- opening the preserved 2025 blind partition;
- a program to qualify every historical stage code.

## Reopen policy

Stage-specific research may be reopened only when a concrete downstream expert or multi-expert use
case exposes a bounded gap, or when genuinely new admissible evidence changes an existing claim
boundary. Any such reopen must start with a new value question and preregistered evidence/stop rules.

The following are explicitly insufficient reasons to reopen the phase:

- making the 0..12 taxonomy complete or symmetrical;
- rescuing a parked label because it has not passed;
- searching more historical sources solely to obtain a desired result;
- rerunning consumed partitions;
- tuning after observing an earlier outcome.

Future prospective receipt/version evidence applies only from the time collection begins and cannot
retroactively repair the 2020 strict-PIT gap.

## Program handoff

Stock-stage-specific P0XX exploration stops at this boundary. The parent Quanthus program may now use
Continuous Facts and the optional Layered Proposition surface when designing or validating the
multi-expert decision chain. This acceptance does not itself authorize that next program phase.

No automatic Stage successor is opened.
