## Question and bounded purpose

Does one existing volume factor change an actual daily research-review queue, how much of
the price-only queue does it exclude, and what review capacity and machine cost does it use?
This is a concrete operational use. It is not a strategy, state classifier, investor return
experiment or independent test of market truth. No position, order, entry or sell exists here.
The old P3 and state requests and their failed/unperformed gates remain immutable.

The fixed development population is the already P10-qualified128 new listings in2021 from
their real first listing through2021-12-31. The consumer explicitly accepts the existing
retrospective unadjusted-coordinate limitation. Historical knowledge availability is unknown;
T-close is an observation/evaluation coordinate and is not fabricated acquisition or knowledge.
No new raw source qualification, source archive hunt, acquisition, training or P6 activation.

## Shared prerequisites and input identity

The DAG is qualified P10 subset -> native admission -> one immutable job-local Facts TMP ->
one volume factor sidecar -> two actual queue arms and an independent arithmetic check.
The existing Structure v2 prior20-high measurement and activity ratio engines each run once.
Both arms share the same Facts, source identity, calendar, action-coordinate resets and full
security/day denominator, including unknowns, unavailable history and suspended observations.
The TMP is not a new ADM. Formal independent reproduction is a separate Job which rebuilds it.

Bind the exact P10 handoff, selection and required native member hashes, code/config closure,
definition version, physical budget, calendar, source revisions and coordinate boundaries.
Do not read unrelated economic terms, old labels, candidate predictions or protected years.
Retain actual source timestamps; no snapshot label upgrades the consumer to historical PIT.

## Unified asset and additive contract

Keep the strict existing eight-definition FeatureRegistry byte-identical. The catalog sidecar
gives each feature/version one canonical identity and business-domain views. The activity
implementation is an alias of `liquidity.volume_ratio20@1.0.0`, not a duplicate new factor asset.
Its formation is qualified volume -> mean of prior20 sessions excluding T -> current/mean.
The catalog distinguishes numerical feature, underlying Facts and consumer decision; being
numeric is not evidence of market validity. It does not create three mutually exclusive
objective/algorithm/qualitative libraries or require every category to be populated.

This retrospective sidecar binds the unchanged registry definition, source Fact revision and
unknown knowledge cutoff. It is not a legacy `FeatureFact`, `PublishedSnapshot` or PIT lineage.
Models are not dependencies; cycles or inference about model/state qualification are absent.

## Two frozen consumer arms

The common price pool is T close strictly above the prior20 high, excluding T. This is a
continuous geometric condition, not a new accepted state3/4 label. Baseline queues that pool.
The candidate admits only its rows with `volume_ratio20 >= 1`, meaning at least the prior mean.
The one threshold is frozen before values under this new policy, not tuned to the result.
Both arms rank by the same frozen security/date hash and use at most five slots per day.
No volume reranking, filler rows, manual override, or post-result choice of a better threshold.

A missing or nonpositive prior mean is unknown, not zero; current zero volume can be a valid
zero ratio. Unknown activity abstains from the candidate while remaining in the denominator.
Missing/invalid bars and coordinate/calendar breaks reset and rewarm the existing Facts.
None of these conditions means structural damage or authorizes a forced sale.

## Acceptance and interpretation

Verify formula correctness independently of queue value: exact Decimal prior20 arithmetic,
source-field timestamps, repeated-key rejection, T-only windows, resets, zero/unknown
distinction and stable revisions. Preserve the old Registry and frozen policy hashes.

Execute and persist both full daily queues. Report actual used/unused slots, price-pool
exclusions, retained/removed/replacement selections, unknowns and per-stage machine time,
RSS, bytes and build/reuse counts. Equal maxima do not mean equal actual review costs.
Report price-only coverage sacrificed alongside any workload reduction. A filter selecting
more rows which satisfy its own definition is not independent evidence of predictive value.
No measured human minutes exist; do not invent them or make an economic-value claim.

Rebuild in an independent new process from an independently copied tiny source subset, with
old sources and network unreachable. Compare full Facts and queue semantic digests. Damage
only a disposable copy and require fail-closed. Check applicable regressions, lint/type and
`make check`; append scoped engineering, quality, value and admission outcomes to the blueprint.
