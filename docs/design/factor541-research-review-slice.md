## Question and bounded purpose

Does one existing volume factor change an actual daily research-review queue, how much of
the price-only queue does it exclude, and what review capacity and machine cost does it use?
This is a concrete operational use. It is not a strategy, state classifier, investor return
experiment or independent test of market truth. No position, order, entry or sell exists here.
The old P3 and state requests and their failed/unperformed gates remain immutable.

The fixed development population is the already P10-qualified128 new listings in2021 from
their real first listing through2021-12-31. The consumer explicitly accepts the existing
retrospective unadjusted-coordinate limitation. Historical knowledge availability is unknown;
T-close is an observation/evaluation coordinate and is not fabricated acquisition or knowledge.
No new raw source qualification, source archive hunt, acquisition, training or P6 activation.

## Two frozen consumer arms

The common price pool is T close strictly above the prior20 high, excluding T. This is a
continuous geometric condition, not a new accepted state3/4 label. Baseline queues that pool.
The candidate admits only its rows with `volume_ratio20 >= 1`, meaning at least the prior mean.
The one threshold is frozen before values under this new policy, not tuned to the result.
Both arms rank by the same frozen security/date hash and use at most five slots per day.
No volume reranking, filler rows, manual override, or post-result choice of a better threshold.

A missing or nonpositive prior mean is unknown, not zero; current zero volume can be a valid
zero ratio. Unknown activity abstains from the candidate while remaining in the denominator.
Missing/invalid bars and coordinate/calendar breaks reset and rewarm the existing Facts.
None of these conditions means structural damage or authorizes a forced sale.

## Owner-approved bounded usefulness diagnostic

The2026-10-28 continuation authorizes one additional retrospective diagnostic under
`config/research/factor541-five-session-diagnostic.v1.json`, within the3-hour task budget.
The old queue contract, factor threshold and records remain immutable. This is previously
exposed2021 development data, not a fresh independent test or historical PIT reconstruction.

The purpose is to reduce research-review entries which quickly fall back below the breakout
reference. A quick failure means at least one valid close strictly below T's frozen prior20
high in the next five exchange sessions. Equality is not failure. All five sessions must be
present, admitted and in the same listing/price coordinate. Any action boundary, unavailable
session or incomplete2021 tail yields unknown; no2022 data is read and unknowns retain their
denominator. The future observations score the diagnostic only, never enter T's features.
The diagnostic also requires the exact handoff digest named in its frozen scope before
source access; a same-census replacement cannot silently become its input.

The primary conditional-capacity control takes the first N original price/hash selections
on each date, where N is that date's candidate count. It is a same-count diagnostic, not an
independently deployable quota policy. Common observations cancel in the paired difference;
changed unknowns give worst/best-case bounds. Security-cluster bootstrap and leave-one-security/
date sensitivity expose dependence; daily slots must not be counted as independent evidence.
The frozen continuation rule requires a positive worst-case reduction, positive bootstrap
lower bound and positive leave-one-security/date nets. Otherwise park the volume gate and
retain the engineering asset. Even a limited positive diagnostic grants no strategy or P6 use.

Reuse anchors from the first Structure build, the same native admissions and immutable Facts.
Record the isolated activity-engine component and five alternating queue timing pairs; the
latter excludes common prerequisites and does not identify total factor cost or human time.
