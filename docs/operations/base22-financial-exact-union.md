# Isolated financial history union

The legacy year union still rejects equal clocks. The separate v2 contract handles
retained observations whose availability clocks coincide because both precede a
known company identity's availability. Two acquisition times remain distinct.
Equal projected numbers alone are insufficient: compare the complete original and
current provider report bodies, source/revision and original dated parent mapping.
Original whole envelopes may request different numbers of periods; preserve both
complete byte closures rather than calling their byte hashes identical.

The dependency graph is original/current raw + fixed dated identity mapping → one
immutable publication/equivalence bridge → affected year outputs → global financial
layout verification → independent relocated cold verification. The bridge is built
once inside the job. A formal cold process independently rederives it once and
shares it with all year audits. No stage consumes a final August image.

For every selected report/fact year, preserve original typed captures as independent
local evidence, exclude only proven duplicate incoming versions, and stream the
remaining typed rows. Keep all scalar types/precision, genuine instants, provenance
and original version clocks. The two allowed field changes are exact next-version
interval closure and changing an original date-only publication bound to unknown
instant. Preserve the original date token/bound and record the real governance
repair time in the scope evidence. A source conflict or absent raw body blocks the
partition. The three separately approved turnover holds grant no financial/cash
priority.

The caller must pin `base22-financial-exact-union.v2.json`, all model/code digests,
complete raw and typed member inventories, parent identities, selection and capacity
before writing. Call `bridge_rows` once to materialize the shared proof; independently
call `verify_bridge` during offline acceptance. `materialize_year` and `audit_year`
compare all typed atoms using that pinned proof, with only key/clock/ordinal TMP.
`audit_financial_layout` supplies full global keys, parents, periods and physical
partition checks beyond those year comparisons. All paths passed to cold functions
must resolve inside the new entity; archived source paths are strings only.

Archive copies and the bridge belong to the job's TMP scope and may be transferred
once into a future complete image under exact ownership receipts. Account for them,
all failed attempts, output, sort spill and cold copies in the device-specific budget.
A failed job never gains READY. Raw proof, typed comparison, relocation, and complete
33-table/PIT/consumer acceptance are separate gates. This module changes no existing
Base, CURRENT, frozen visibility policy or accepted research receipt.
