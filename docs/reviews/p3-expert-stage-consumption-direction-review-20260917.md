## Decision

The meeting unanimously selected the demand-discovery route rather than Stage 3/4, Stage 12/1,
Stage 2, or another vocabulary-expansion route. The next bounded product question is whether the
already accepted Stage10/11 development semantic surface is useful and sufficient when consumed by
Quanthus expert roles, and which concrete semantic gap, if any, appears during that consumption.

This does not reopen `stock-stage-research-phase-1`. It is an Expert Strategy Layer consumer
increment. Continuous Facts remain the authoritative computational/evidence substrate. The Layered
Stage Proposition surface remains optional, development-only, retrospective for the frozen P029
population, and cannot alter score, veto, constraint, action, strategy authority, or trading
permission in this scope.

## Shared rationale

All four reviews independently reached the same conclusion:

- Stage10/11 are the only accepted development projections and already have real-history P029 replay.
- P033 showed a strong current-package interpretation benefit but did not establish an
  information-equivalent Facts control, so pure presentation causality is still unproven.
- Stage 3/4 have strong development evidence but their one external arbitration route stopped before
  semantic scoring; Stage 12/1 remain parent-reference limited; Stage 2 remains exploratory.
- None of those candidate families currently has a concrete downstream expert-consumption gap that
  satisfies the phase-1 reopen rule.
- Therefore the next information-gain step is to consume Stage10/11 and let actual role needs expose
  whether another semantic family is required.

## Bounded successor objective

`P3-EXPERT-006` may prepare and evaluate one research-only expert-consumption packet family over the
exact P029 ten-observation population. Every role sees the same ten observations with authoritative
Continuous Facts and the accepted Stage10/11 development-shadow overlay.

The phase asks only:

1. can the role correctly interpret the parent structural-failure context and nested rebound;
2. is the existing surface sufficient for bounded advisory interpretation in that role;
3. what advisory use, if any, the role can make of it without action authority;
4. what missing semantic family, if any, repeatedly blocks useful interpretation; and
5. whether the role can consume the surface while preserving the Facts/semantic authority boundary.

A future Stage-specific proposal requires a concrete recurring gap. Taxonomy completeness, maturity
of a candidate code, or the desire to qualify all 12 historical states is not sufficient.

## Gap trigger

A named missing semantic family is a candidate downstream gap only when at least two independent
roles select the same non-`none` family across at least three distinct observation identities in
total. `other` requires explicit four-party manual review and never auto-opens research. No outcome
automatically creates a Stage successor.

## Stop conditions

Stop and reconvene before continuing if execution requires any of the following:

- changing Stage10/11 semantics, thresholds, hierarchy, evidence grade, or `primary_stage`;
- new market/DWD reads or protected 2022-2024 / 2025 access;
- historical PIT/source archaeology;
- action-changing expert mappings, score, veto, constraints, aggregation, position policy, or P2;
- prediction, economic, OOS, production, Shadow, strategy, notification, or trading claims;
- a different evaluator model/provider after any result is visible.

The phase closes after one frozen run and four-role result review, regardless of whether a gap is
found.
