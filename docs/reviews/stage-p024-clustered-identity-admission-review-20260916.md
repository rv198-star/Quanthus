## Short-term trading perspective

**Decision: keep early STAR as a separate regime, but stop treating 24 as a semantic truth.**

The first STAR regime is short by construction and has materially different market rules from later
STAR and contemporaneous MAIN/ChiNext. Removing or merging it would erase exactly the time × board
interaction the resource model was created to preserve.

Nineteen distinct names inside that cell are still useful for a bounded semantic stress test. They are
not enough to claim market prevalence or the same cell-level precision as a 24-row cell. Adding five
same-cell repeated measures merely to restore a square matrix would add path observations without
adding issuer diversity and would make the sample look cleaner than it is.

**Veto:** do not merge/drop early STAR, do not manufacture five repeated same-cell observations, and do
not call 19 equivalent to 24.
