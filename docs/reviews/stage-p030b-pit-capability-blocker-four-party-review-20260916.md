## Frozen blocker reviewed

P030 targeted strict `PUBLIC_RECONSTRUCTION` / `counterfactual_recomputation` for the exact ten P029
Stage10-active observations at T 15:30 Asia/Shanghai. Stage10/11 semantics, thresholds, parent
relationship, `primary_stage=null`, development-shadow authority and the 10/10 pass denominator were
frozen.

The capability audit found a decisive daily-bar lineage gap before any Stage PIT predicate was run:

- current F9/Stage source is explicitly `retrospective_dwd_record_view`;
- accepted ODS PIT evidence says historical daily-bar acquisition time is absent and retained
  `built_at` belongs to the 2026 package construction period;
- session close is only a governed economic-availability lower bound;
- current authority does not bind a complete 2020 per-session source revision / receipt family;
- existing temporal contracts require complete revision-family and availability evidence for strict
  public reconstruction;
- the earlier P4 session-close projection remains explicitly retrospective with
  `per_decision_publication_proven=false`.

No Stage PIT result was computed, no observation was removed or replaced, no Stage threshold or
semantic rule was changed, and no protected partition was opened.

## Short-term / event-driven review

**Blocker:** sufficient. Missing historical version/publication evidence is an input-admission
failure, not something the Stage predicate can repair.

**Selected route:** close P030 blocked and open a separate historical source-version evidence
feasibility route. Do not interpret the feasibility route as a promise to restart P030.

The reviewer recommended a bounded search with a small fixed source list and explicit end state. A
qualifying source must prove the complete version family and public time for the exact required
historical inputs. `session_close`, current download time, build time or current-file existence are
not substitutes.

## Fund / portfolio-risk review

**Blocker:** sufficient. Continuing would risk injecting later corrections into a purported
historical decision state and would overstate the risk process' contemporaneous knowledge.

**Selected route:** close P030 blocked and investigate one independently authoritative historical
EOD source family under a small fixed resource budget. Success grants source-candidate status only;
it does not alter portfolio decisions, risk limits, Stage authority or the 10/10 PIT gate.

If the required source version was only available after 15:30, the original cutoff must not be moved
to rescue the experiment.

## Quant research review

**Blocker:** sufficient as an identification failure. `10/10` is a reconstruction-completeness gate,
not a statistical significance threshold. No PIT signal result exists and the blocker must not be
reported as a negative signal result.

**Selected route:** one separate evidence-feasibility experiment. The minimum evidence unit is:

> source authority × security × trading date × required field set × exact source version × public
> availability evidence.

Candidate ordering and scope must be fixed before evidence collection. Search must not use Stage
labels, retrospective agreement or economic outcome to choose sources or versions. Partial coverage
is descriptive only and cannot pass.

## Data-governance / warehouse review

**Blocker:** sufficient. Strict PIT requires a governed source-version family and authenticated
availability evidence; a canonical/latest historical value plus session completion is not enough.

**Selected route:** a separately governed source-candidate feasibility. A qualifying candidate must
retain source authority, payload/version identity, revision/correction lineage, distinct publication
and acquisition clocks, raw evidence hashes and auditable mapping. A historical private receipt does
not automatically prove PUBLIC availability, and a receipt without source-version binding is also
insufficient.

Success ends at source-candidate status. Integration and any Stage/PIT rerun require another review.

## Consensus decision

All four independent reviews selected the same route:

1. close P030 as `BLOCKED_SOURCE_VERSION_OR_AVAILABILITY_LINEAGE`;
2. preserve the strict PIT definition and all Stage10/11 semantics;
3. open one bounded successor whose sole purpose is historical source-version evidence feasibility;
4. use the exact P029 population/dependency need as the evidence target but do **not** execute Stage;
5. success may only produce a candidate source contract;
6. source integration, selector changes or a future P030-equivalent replay require a fresh four-party
   decision.

The successor search is narrowed to **one source family**. The first and only family is the primary
listing-exchange official EOD archive family, represented by the venue-specific official authorities
needed for the frozen securities. There is no rolling expansion to commercial aggregators if this
family fails.

## Success and stop conditions for the successor

A source-candidate feasibility PASS requires evidence that the fixed historical dependency scope can
be supported by:

- authoritative source identity and stable product/data identity;
- exact values/fields for the required securities and trading dates;
- historical revision/correction family or another independently justified completeness proof;
- authenticated public availability time/precision sufficient to decide the T=15:30 cutoff;
- preserved raw payload/checksum/evidence references and a reproducible field mapping;
- explicit gaps/conflicts; no inferred arrival time or latest-value fallback.

Stop the successor and close no-pass if the official exchange family provides only today's corrected
historical value, cannot establish historical version completeness, cannot establish publication-time
semantics relevant to 15:30, or cannot cover the frozen dependency requirement without changing the
experiment.

Search failure means only that this preregistered authority family did not establish feasibility; it
does not prove no source exists anywhere.

## Mandatory re-review triggers

Stop and reconvene all four roles before continuing if any proposal would:

- add a second source family after the official-exchange family fails;
- use commercial/provider-derived history, proxy securities or cross-source row-by-row value
  selection;
- change the ten observations, dependency window, T=15:30 cutoff or 10/10 standard;
- change Stage10/11 semantics, thresholds, parent relation or `primary_stage=null`;
- weaken source-version completeness, availability evidence or public-reconstruction rules;
- access 2022-2024 arbitration or 2025 blind data;
- execute Stage labels, predictive/economic scoring or strategy/trading logic inside source
  feasibility;
- move from source-candidate feasibility to actual source integration or PIT replay.

## Claim ceiling

This review establishes that P030 must close at a source-version/availability evidence blocker and
that one bounded source-evidence feasibility successor is justified. It does not establish that an
official exchange archive is sufficient, that strict PIT will later pass, or that Stage10/11 has any
predictive or economic value.
