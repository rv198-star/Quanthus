## Quantitative perspective

The third group should not begin with six independent label searches. `0/2`, `1/12`, and `8/9`
encode materially different statistical questions. A continuous profile is the smallest admissible
next step. The profile must retain correlated daily rows as correlated observations, avoid future
outcomes, and refuse to optimize thresholds from aggregate distributions.

The proposed direction-efficiency, range-width/center, position, velocity and volume measures are
acceptable as job-local diagnostic quantities. Their presence in a profile is not evidence that they
should all become formal Facts or model features.

## Short-term trading perspective

`8 加速赶顶` and `9 高位分歧/派发` contain interpretive language that can easily become hindsight or
fund-intent claims. The observable content should be tested as **upside acceleration/extension** and
**high-level price-volume divergence** first. It is plausible that both are modifiers of an advancing
context rather than peer primary stages.

`1 止跌企稳` must mean current deceleration/stabilization evidence, not "the low is in". `12 下行推进`
must be observable continuing decline. A later new low or reversal cannot be used to relabel T.

## Fund perspective

For medium-horizon use, continuous structure is likely more reusable than a forced six-state layer.
The profile should emphasize reusable order, compression, direction, drawdown/rebound and activity
quantities. Stage 0 must be positively evidenced disorder; Stage 2 must not encode hidden
"accumulation" intent. High-side acceleration/divergence is particularly likely to be context or risk
annotation rather than a portfolio state by itself.
