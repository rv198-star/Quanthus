## Decision and allocation

Pause Head2 research investment now. Retain the seven-feature representation,
checkpoints, Facts, fit/update primitives and complete-capital comparison. Stop
this entry-filter experiment with its negative result. Do not tune 0.5, add more
features, move the cutoff, relabel consumed samples or search for another cohort
to reverse that result. No P6 run is justified for this rejected candidate.

The scarce resource is the next **four hours of agent/script work**, not the number
of labels completed. The safety floor is preserved sources/checkpoints, meaningful
regression coverage, original consumer vetoes, scoped retrospective data boundaries
and measured free space. This proposal does not schedule new raw acquisition or
source repair. The review observed system free space only slightly above the old
8 GiB floor: measure it again before writing, avoid another full worktree/data copy,
and stop only the affected write if its declared margin does not fit.

Steps 1–2 share their final check run. Step 3 can inspect existing metadata while
those checks execute; this is not authority to spawn parallel research trials or
consume the same holdout in several experiments. The ceilings sum to four hours;
the expected wall time is **roughly 3–4 hours**, excluding any external CI/account
block. Do not fill unused budget with new experimentation. Integration may uncover
dependency conflicts; keep a concrete reviewed patch and precise remaining merge
work if the ceiling is reached. Do not lower tests or blindly merge to hit the time.

The preferred delivery is the already-tested common Facts/reader/fit primitives
and comparison boundary. A parked experiment's specialized driver does not need
another maintenance campaign merely to make every file look accepted. Its P2 blocks
reuse of that entry, not delivery of unrelated qualified code. Repair it only if it
belongs in the chosen callable closure; the original experiment remains archived
and reproducible at its recorded identity either way.

Step 2 uses existing successful branch evidence. It does not recopy all six models,
rebuild ten years of features or rerun every previous experiment. Check only actual
target-branch compatibility changes, plus the required repository checks. Publishing
or merging follows the Owner's applicable task authority; this proposed route by
itself is not a new merge or deployment instruction.

Step 3 is a **development diagnosis**, not an independent confirmation. Start with
the already-consumed #545 population: 538 entry episodes / 8,393 held reviews across
343 issuers, preserving the 3,029 unavailable structure observations. Freeze its
complete population and exact existing trace/Facts identities before result access.
Do not extend to previously unread B issuers, open new market rows or rebuild the
old source pipeline. Entry diagnosis may use only the separately inventoried,
already-consumed 790-case diagnostic or March87 if its exact reuse is frozen first;
it is optional and must fit the same hour, not an excuse for a wider census.

Do not pick losing trades or winning alternative thresholds and call them a new
validation set. March87 are now consumed. The old 975/87 inventory counts
cannot be advertised as remaining independent sample capacity without an updated
exposure ledger. Existing high idle cash in this narrow experiment alone does not
establish either a ranking/capacity problem or its absence in the wider system.

The diagnosis is about the existing algorithm's decisions and permissible actions.
The earlier proposed human-priority queue lacked a verified need and remains parked;
do not rename it. A discrepancy in an output is not automatically a fixable opportunity:
the alternative must have usable evidence at the decision cutoff and must not be
redundant with an existing expert/veto. Missing inputs remain unavailable and never
force a sale. Shared source defects route to P10; no archive hunt in this batch.
This is not another vote on the old 10/11 mapping: that mapping's one changed episode
remains a closed negative result. The deliverable is a reason-coded census of the
baseline's decisions and unresolved needs; if the existing trace cannot support it
without reopening sources, report that exact limit and stop at the hour ceiling.

## Conditional steps 4–5

At the end of step 3, choose exactly one branch:

1. **No material, repeatable, permissible gap:** finish the asset delivery and park
   the learning campaign. Report the remaining product-MVP qualification honestly;
   do not declare report-only or engineering assets a complete adopted learning
   product. Keep normal project delivery moving without forcing a new state head.
2. **Continuous Facts/simple rule can address the gap:** prefer a versioned,
   bounded #545 consumer candidate with C0 plus one simple alternative. No state
   model is obligatory. Before execution, freeze significance/materiality rules,
   costs, exact information timing and untouched evaluation capacity. If the
   simple tool succeeds, accept only that use, not a learned-model claim.
3. **A distinct learnable state is genuinely needed:** name the state from the
   problem, not from the next number in 1..12. First qualify source/reference and
   train/update/evaluation separation under #542; then at most one candidate and
   one local update under #543/#544. Head3/4, 10/11 or Head2 require new evidence of
   the specific missing capability; existing failed maps are not automatically reopened.

Step 4 is that frozen qualification/selection, with an initial **one-hour ceiling**.
If a qualified comparison is possible, step 5 has a proposed **2–3 hour ceiling**
for one actual consumer comparison and required independent reproduction, using
the existing bounded capital path only when trading decisions change. These are
conditional additional budgets, not an already-started task or a promise of gain.
Need for new sources, protected years, unclear reference meaning or a material
sample/target change is surfaced before execution, not after selecting unusable
samples. Evidence failure stops that branch; no sample or threshold rescue.

Only qualified, material decision benefit merits candidate-specific P6 simulation.
Negative/inconclusive comparisons can close their research task and retain the old
version. They do not automatically close every MVP gate or prevent independently
useful components from being delivered.

## Blueprint and completion

For each delivered tranche update only its actual module/facet through the ledger:
TRAIN/REPRO/CLOSEOUT for boundary repair and asset delivery; EXPERT for the actual
decision diagnosis; one state module only if its later qualification changes.
Keep Head2's research-only quality, rejected entry value and ungranted admission.
Use accepted/rejected/parked evidence, not issue counts, to report progress. No
new overall percentage is derived from the number of completed heads.

The first tranche ends with: a repaired reusable entry or explicit exclusion of the
parked driver, a precise main-target Git
handoff, the six-subitem closure matrix, one problem card or explicit no-go, actual
checks/costs and an updated blueprint. It must not end merely with another general
recommendation to “inspect the opportunity later.”
